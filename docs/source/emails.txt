Vessel Chartering for Break Bulk & Project Cargo

Vessel chartering for break bulk and project cargo provides flexible, efficient transport for oversized, heavy, or irregular goods that cannot fit into standard containers. CGS specializes in custom vessel chartering to ensure complex industrial shipments reach their destinations safely, on time, and according to exact project schedules. 
Our Vessel Chartering Services
Tailored vessel solutions match your cargo size with the right ship. Options range from small feeder ships to large ocean-crossing vessels, often combining barges, landing craft transport (LCT), and deep-sea mother vessels to move cargo from local ports to global destinations. 
How Tailored Vessel Solutions Work
Cargo Assessment: Matching weight, volume, and type to the correct ship size.
Barge and LCT Operations: Using shallow-draft vessels or landing craft to load cargo where large ships cannot dock.
Mother Vessel Connection: Transferring goods from local barges to large deep-sea vessels for long-distance travel.
Port-to-Port Delivery: Managing the full route from initial commercial ports to final international destinations. 
Route Selection: Experts study routes and use trusted partners to ensure smooth port transit and lower costs.
Onboard Cargo Handling: Crews use specialized gear and skills to load, store, and unload fragile or massive items safely.
Compliance and Documentation: The team manages all global shipping rules, customs papers, and port permits. 
Why Choose CGS
 Comprehensive Cargo Handling and Marine Logistics Solutions
Managing heavy or specialized cargo requires an end-to-end approach that accounts for every technical and logistical hurdle from origin to destination. The process begins with the initial fabrication and custom packaging of boxes designed to withstand harsh transit conditions. Securing cargo for ocean travel demands sea-worthy packing standards that protect assets against moisture, shifting, and environmental wear during long voyages.
Engineering precision remains vital throughout the preparation phase. Specialists perform detailed lifting and lashing calculations to verify that weight distributions and anchor points can endure dynamic ocean forces. Teams coordinate pre-approval stages for the stowage plan while ensuring certified lifting gears are fully available and ready for deployment.
Operational success also relies heavily on transparent commercial and contractual alignment. Coordinators review every clause and requirement alongside ship owners and charterers to eliminate operational friction. Finally, the service oversees the entire transit phase down to the final drop-off location, ensuring a secure and seamless delivery.
