## Service page 1 — Project Freight Forwarding

Supplied verbatim by the client (email: *"In Service tab Project freight
forwarding page"*, sent twice with identical content).

> Project freight forwarding requires choosing the right mode of transport based
> on cargo size, urgency, budget, and site constraints. Companies like CGS
> analyze packing lists to deliver cost-effective, practical solutions instead of
> pushing expensive charters.

### Transport Solutions

- **Container Vessels** — Best for standard over-sized or heavy components that fit safely inside standard or high-cube containers.
- **Mafi Trailers** — Ideal for rolling heavy or awkward rolling stock and breakbulk units directly onto vessels via ramp.
- **Specialized Equipment** — Utilizes flat racks, platforms, open-top, and over-height containers for odd dimensions.
- **Tugs and Barges** — Useful for remote job sites or shallow waters lacking deep-water port infrastructure.
- **Geared/Semi-Geared Vessels** — Independent lifting gear allows loading and discharging at ports without shore cranes.

### The CGS Approach

- **Packing List Analysis** — Matches cargo dimensions directly with equipment capabilities.
- **Cost Efficiency** — Balances urgency against commercial constraints to avoid overspending.
- **Feasibility Studies** — Ensures routes, handling gear, and vessel types match project needs.

**Note:** the five "Transport Solutions" bullets double as internal links —
Mafi Trailers → Roll On/Roll Off, Tugs and Barges → Tug & Barge Chartering,
Geared/Semi-Geared → Heavy Lift Chartering.

## Service page 2 — Chartering Heavy Lift & Semi-Geared Vessels

Supplied verbatim by the client (email: *"Breakbulk chartering -1 pics"*). The
client's own heading for this page is **"Breakbulk and project cargo chartering
on self geared and semi geared vessels"**.

### Vessel Chartering for Break Bulk & Project Cargo

> Vessel chartering for break bulk and project cargo provides flexible, efficient
> transport for oversized, heavy, or irregular goods that cannot fit into standard
> containers. CGS specializes in custom vessel chartering to ensure complex
> industrial shipments reach their destinations safely, on time, and according to
> exact project schedules.

### Our Vessel Chartering Services

> Tailored vessel solutions match your cargo size with the right ship. Options
> range from small feeder ships to large ocean-crossing vessels, often combining
> barges, landing craft transport (LCT), and deep-sea mother vessels to move cargo
> from local ports to global destinations.

### How Tailored Vessel Solutions Work

- **Cargo Assessment** — Matching weight, volume, and type to the correct ship size.
- **Barge and LCT Operations** — Using shallow-draft vessels or landing craft to load cargo where large ships cannot dock.
- **Mother Vessel Connection** — Transferring goods from local barges to large deep-sea vessels for long-distance travel.
- **Port-to-Port Delivery** — Managing the full route from initial commercial ports to final international destinations.
- **Route Selection** — Experts study routes and use trusted partners to ensure smooth port transit and lower costs.
- **Onboard Cargo Handling** — Crews use specialized gear and skills to load, store, and unload fragile or massive items safely.
- **Compliance and Documentation** — The team manages all global shipping rules, customs papers, and port permits.

### Why Choose CGS — Comprehensive Cargo Handling and Marine Logistics Solutions

> Managing heavy or specialized cargo requires an end-to-end approach that
> accounts for every technical and logistical hurdle from origin to destination.
> The process begins with the initial fabrication and custom packaging of boxes
> designed to withstand harsh transit conditions. Securing cargo for ocean travel
> demands sea-worthy packing standards that protect assets against moisture,
> shifting, and environmental wear during long voyages.
>
> Engineering precision remains vital throughout the preparation phase.
> Specialists perform detailed lifting and lashing calculations to verify that
> weight distributions and anchor points can endure dynamic ocean forces. Teams
> coordinate pre-approval stages for the stowage plan while ensuring certified
> lifting gears are fully available and ready for deployment.
>
> Operational success also relies heavily on transparent commercial and
> contractual alignment. Coordinators review every clause and requirement
> alongside ship owners and charterers to eliminate operational friction. Finally,
> the service oversees the entire transit phase down to the final drop-off
> location, ensuring a secure and seamless delivery.

That last block is prose, but it lists four discrete capabilities — fabrication
and sea-worthy packing, lifting/lashing calculations, stowage plan and certified
gear, contractual review — which is how it should be laid out on the page.

## Service page 5 — Air Freight

Supplied verbatim by the client (email: *"Services page by Air Page"*, 7
photographs attached — client's own note says more are available on request
once the page is written).

> Smart air freight goes beyond booking costly full charters. CGS works closely
> with clients to understand their needs, optimize cargo packing, and use
> multi-modal transport to cut shipping costs.

### The High Cost of Convenience

> Full wide-body aircraft charters offer speed but come with massive financial
> and resource burdens. When direct uplift is unavailable or prohibitively
> expensive, supply chain professionals turn to clever routing and line-haul
> trucking.

### Creative Solutions Over Direct Charters

- **Cross-Border European Hauls** — Moving goods by truck from remote regions in Norway down to major aviation hubs like Amsterdam avoids special charter costs while tapping into regular wide-body departures.
- **Southeast Asian Multi-Modal Legs** — Transporting freight from Singapore hubs up to Kuching, followed by secondary long-distance trucking to regional destinations like Bintulu and Miri, bypasses the need for restricted or expensive local air connections.

### Benefits of Smart Multimodal Routing

- Drastically lower operational costs compared to dedicated charter flights.
- Maximized use of existing scheduled wide-body flight capacity.
- Flexible adaptation to regional airport infrastructure limits.

> Our experts will study your details and build a fast, budget-friendly plan.
